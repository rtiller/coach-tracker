## v0.1.4.2-preview (2026-10-27)

The inner portrait home after browsing a week (the owner's report: a duplicate day under Week and progress, and the top card cut off).

- **Root cause, in plain words.** The layout (phone, inner portrait or two panes) was decided when the page started and re-checked only on a resize, a turn, an unfold, or 720ms after focus left a control. On the owner's Home Screen launch the window reported a phone-sized viewport for a moment and no resize followed, so the home stayed in the phone layout (no Up next, Week and progress half width). Opening and closing Week and progress moves focus, so the next re-check switched the home to inner portrait in the middle of the visit. That brought in the Up next card, and Up next was worked out for whatever week was on screen, not for today, so it showed "Up next · Tue" under the browsed week's Monday (the "duplicate day"). Once shown, it was also never taken away when its target went away (the fit code returned early without re-rendering). The extra height then set off the "keep the end in view" code: a reader at the top of a page that only just overflows counted as "at the end", so the page scrolled the whole gap (88px at 669x951) and the card's top, with Skip, went under the status bar.
- **The layout settles at launch.** The head script also re-checks on load, pageshow, a return to the page, a visual viewport resize, and on a short series of timers in the first 2.5 seconds, so the home comes up in the right layout without waiting for a focus change. Text fields still freeze it, as before.
- **Up next only on the normal Today home.** It shows only when no day is picked and the week on screen is the current week (the plan's calendar week). Picking a day or browsing another week hides it, and it is re-rendered (hidden) every time, so a card made for the home never stays under a browsed week. With no day picked its target is always after the day the card shows, so it never names the shown day. Where the design shows it (spec (d): the inner portrait home, Today, strip, Up next, Week and progress), it is unchanged; gate-v014 (d) still gates it.
- **No scroll past the top limit.** When the bottom group changes height while the reader is at the end, the page still keeps the end in view, but never scrolls the Today card's top above the top limit the day pick already uses (the scroller's scroll-padding-top: the safe top plus the clearance). So Skip is never under the status bar or cut off.
- **Version:** About and the file's head comment read v0.1.4.2-preview.
- **Gates:** new `test/gate-v0142.js` (both engines): the owner's flow (open Week and progress, week 5 on the spine, leave) and strip picks, at 669x951 Home Screen app (safe 82/0/34), the same at 200% text, 669x867, 669x751 and 720x951, each from a clean launch and from a launch whose viewport reads 466x678 for its first 300ms. It fails on a5966af and passes on this build. `test/gate-about.js` now checks the exact version in About and in the head comment (was: any string starting with "v" and a digit). ## v0.1.0-preview (2026-10-24)

- An empty state for a device with no plan.
- Storage keys use the `ct1-` prefix.
- `tools/encode-plan.js` (no dependencies), `PLAN-SCHEMA.md`, `plan.schema.json`, five sample plans.
