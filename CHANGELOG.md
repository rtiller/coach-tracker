## v0.1.3.4-preview (2026-10-26)

From the 200% text review of the Counter (the cover screen in landscape) and the About copy. Storage schema unchanged (`ct1-`, schema 1), no keys added, no new data. The CSP is byte-identical to v0.1.3.3; escaping and storage code unchanged; no network code added.

- **Counter at 200% text showed one exercise and blank space (root cause).** The whole Today card was the scroller, and the actions bar (Skip, Mark day done and the reserved Undo slot above them) is pinned to the card's bottom with an opaque background. At 644x382 with 200% text the bar took 172px of the 349px card, so the rows scrolled underneath the empty Undo slot. Only about 56px of the list showed (the first line of "Incline bench press"), and the rest of that row and the other three rows sat behind a blank band with nothing saying more was there.
- **Fix: the exercise list is its own scroll area** (`overflow-y: auto`) between the title block and the pinned actions; the card no longer scrolls. The header (kicker, title, focus) and Skip / Mark day done stay put.
- **"N more" pill.** A solid button sits on the list's bottom edge, at the right so the start of each row stays readable, whenever rows are cut off. N is the number of rows not fully visible, and its accessible name is "N more exercises" (or "1 more exercise"). A tap scrolls to the next hidden row (or by a list height when that row is already partly showing). It hides at the end of the list and when nothing overflows; if it had focus, focus moves to Mark day done. It is at least 44x44 and never covers Skip, Done or the Undo slot. No mask, fade or gradient anywhere.
- **Room for the pill at 200% text.** The list keeps 46px when it scrolls, so a two-line day title still leaves room for the 44px pill at 644x382. To fund that, at 200% text only, Skip and Mark day done are 44px tall (were 70px), and the 4px gap above them and the gaps around the list go. The list has 2px of end padding (WebKit scrolls in whole pixels, so the last row's final pixel could not otherwise come into view), and a pill tap that lands within 4px of the end goes to the end. The reserved Undo slot keeps its full height: the Undo message takes two lines at 200%. Nothing changes at normal text.
- **About:** "No accounts, no analytics, no server." is now "No accounts and no analytics." The site does serve the page, so "no server" was not accurate. The README says the same.
- **Page description:** the page had no description of its own, so the host generated one ("A private offline workout tracking tool..."). The app is not an offline app (it needs the page to load), so the head now carries its own `description` and `og:description`: "A private workout tracker that keeps your plan and logs in this browser."
- **Gate (`test/gate-v0133.js`, section 8):** at 644x382 (no inset and 21px) and 678x396 (21px) with 200% text, every training day of every sample: the list scrolls and the card does not; the header and the actions stay whole and on top; the pill shows exactly when rows overflow, with the right N and name; each tap scrolls, N never grows, and it hides only at the end; no mask, filter or gradient on the list, the pill or any ancestor; 44px targets; no overlap with the header, the actions, the Undo slot or the week list. - **Screenshots (`test/screens-v0133.js`):** the longest day at 200% text at 644x382 and 678x396, at rest and after one tap on the pill.
- **Second pass (design must-fix): snap and a strip for the pill, Counter at 200% text with an overflowing list only.** A pill tap could stop mid-row, leaving a title cut off at the list's top and part of the previous row showing. Now the list snaps (`scroll-snap-type: y mandatory`) to each row's top and to the start of every line inside a row. A row taller than the list is not a snap area itself, because browsers let an oversized snap area stop anywhere inside it (measured in both engines); a 1px marker at its top takes that role. Each tap moves exactly one exercise, with its top at the list's top. N counts only rows entirely below the visible area, and the pill hides once the last row is reached. An end padding lets the last line reach the top. Exercise names use line-height 1.25 here, at least the font's content height, so a line's box ends where the next line's snap point starts.

With no overflow there is no strip. To pay for it, the focus line is hidden and the day title's line-height is 1 in this state. A two-line title at 644x382 otherwise left the list less than one line.
- **Gate section 8** adds checks for snap, the row top after every tap, N, the hidden pill at the last row, the pill clear of every row, no cut line at the top (at rest, after taps, at the end and after a scroll settles at four offsets), and the Undo slot clear. Section 6 now reaches a row in a snapping list at the row's own snap point. `scrollIntoView` aims the row's bottom at the list's bottom, and both engines then take the nearest snap point, which can be a line of the row before. Screenshots add the last row and Undo showing.

**Device review fixes (after the first publish of this build).**

- **Inner screen sideways lost the two-pane split (root cause).** The modes came from the browser's reported device screen (`device-width`). On the real phone, Safari can keep reporting the cover screen's size (or a stale size) after unfolding, so the inner screen got the cover layout in both rotations. Emulators always report the inner size, so the gates could not see it. **Fix (rule approved by the maintainer):** the layout now uses only the page's own size (`innerWidth` x `innerHeight`, never `visualViewport` and never the reported screen). The inner layout is on when the short side is at least 520px: wider than tall gives the two panes, otherwise inner portrait. Anything else is the phone layout (the Counter in landscape under 500 tall). It is re-checked on resize, orientation change and unfold, and frozen while a text field has focus and for 700ms after (so the keyboard never flips it). Side effect: an iPad or a desktop window whose short side is 520 or more gets the inner layout.
- **How:** CSS can't freeze a media query, so the layout is classes on `<html>` (`L-duo`, `L-innerp`) set by a small script in the head before first paint. `tools/layout-css.py` turns each layout block in the source (`@media __DUO__ {`, `__COVER__`, `__INNERP__`) into a `:where(html.L-...)` rule at build time, so specificity is unchanged; the build fails if a layout token is left over. `window.LAYOUT` replaces the media-query object in JS.
- **Gate (`test/gate-slab.js`):** inner portrait and inner landscape with the reported screen set to the cover's size or a stale size; opened folded and then unfolded (and rotated and folded again) on one page; every slab iPhone and Android in landscape stays single-column; the keyboard freeze. Mutations: the v0.1.3.2 width rule and the v0.1.3.3 device-screen rule must both fail it.
- **Counter, one surface with two matching panels (design review):** Today (56%) and Week (44%) share the card surface, the 16px radius and the height, with tops and bottoms aligned. Both headers are the same height (44px, or less where 44px would leave a week row under 44px; the header buttons keep a 44px hit area). Today: the ring and the kicker (`W1 of 12 · Block 1`, one line, never cut; it may wrap at 200% text), then the title, the exercises, and Skip and Mark day done at the bottom. The reserved Undo slot sits right above the buttons, inside the panel. Week: the header is "Week and progress" with a chevron (the whole row opens it), with Back to today on the right, its space always kept. One type scale for both panels: names clamp(17px, 0.40 x row, 22px), day labels and sets in mono clamp(13px, 0.30 x row, 16px). Status icons 24px in a 44x44 box. A day that does not fit scrolls inside the panel with the buttons kept in view.
- **About:** a muted line shows the window size, the size the browser reports as the screen, and the layout in use, for checking a device.

**Layout modes: one definition.** `tools/build.sh` holds the media queries once and substitutes them into every `@media` rule and `matchMedia` call (`__COVER__`, `__DUO__`, `__INNERP__`, `__WIDE__`); `OUTER_MQ` in `src/app5.js` is the only JS test. Two panes need two things: a window at least 820px wide and a big device, meaning the screen (`device-width`/`device-height`, the same numbers as `screen.width`/`screen.height`) is at least 600px on its short side (`__INNERDEV__`). A head script sets `html.duo-inner` from the same query before first paint. Every slab phone fails the screen test, so it never gets two panes or the inner density, in either orientation. The screen is the one size a keyboard cannot change, so typing still never flips the layout; window height and aspect ratio stay out of the mode queries. The maintainer approved this rule on 2026-10-25.

## v0.1.0-preview (2026-10-24)

- An empty state for a device with no plan.
- Storage keys use the `ct1-` prefix.
- `tools/encode-plan.js` (no dependencies), `PLAN-SCHEMA.md`, `plan.schema.json`, five sample plans.
